About this study

This was a study on how the framing of a default suggestion affects donation behavior in a payment app. The purchase history, charity, and donation were all hypothetical — nothing was actually charged or donated.

Half of participants saw a generic recommendation, and half saw a recommendation framed as personalized based on their purchases. We're measuring whether this framing changes how much people choose to give.
